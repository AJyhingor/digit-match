Last 3 digits form in the setting
The bot watches the last digit of every tick. When there are at least "In a row" ODD (or EVEN) digits in a row, that is a setting: ODD or EVEN run + the form of the last 3 price digits (for 4.56: X=4, y=5, z=6; forms are X.yz all different, X.yy, X.xx, X.yx, X.xy). It records whether the NEXT tick was odd or even. When the same setting comes again and it was seen at least "Seen before" times, it buys MATCH on the 5 digits (ODD 1 3 5 7 9 or EVEN 0 2 4 6 8) of whichever came next more often. Stakes: 0.35 → 0.75 → 1.75 → 3.90 per digit. A round wins if one of the 5 digits hits; a loss moves up a level, a win returns to level 1.
